## How to Deploy with Custom Domain (Future)

### Step 1: Buy Domain
- Go to GoDaddy, Namecheap, or Google Domains
- Search for "vsnmschool.com" or "vsnmschool.in"
- Purchase for ~$10-15/year

### Step 2: Get Hosting
Options:
- **Vercel** (easiest, free tier available)
- **Netlify** (free for frontend)
- **DigitalOcean** ($5-10/month)
- **AWS** (pay as you go)

### Step 3: Deploy Your Code
1. Download code (GitHub method from COLLEGE_SUBMISSION_GUIDE.md)
2. Push to hosting service
3. Connect your custom domain
4. Update DNS settings

Update frontend/.env:
```

```
